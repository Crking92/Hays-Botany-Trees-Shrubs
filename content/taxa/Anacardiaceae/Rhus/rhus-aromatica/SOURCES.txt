MORPHOLOGY
Low deciduous suckering shrub; exactly three often lobed/coarsely toothed leaflets; early yellowish clusters; red hairy drupes.

JOEY/FAMILY TRUSTED LAYER
The completed Anacardiaceae backfill establishes the local Rhus-vs-Toxicodendron teaching split:
- do not teach "leaves of three = poison ivy";
- Rhus usually has red hairy terminal drupes;
- Toxicodendron has pale axillary drupes and climbing T. radicans may show aerial rootlets;
- do not use sap contact/crushing as an identification test.
