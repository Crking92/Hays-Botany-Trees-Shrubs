## Morphology

https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Ungnadia%20speciosa

Native Plant Society of Texas:
https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/ungnadia-speciosa/

Key learner cues:
- alternate odd-pinnately compound leaves
- pink spring flowers
- distinctive 3-lobed woody capsule
- large shiny dark seeds
- distinguish from Aesculus by alternate pinnate vs opposite palmate leaves
