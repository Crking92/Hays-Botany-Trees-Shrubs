## Morphology
UT PRC Williamson County treatment:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Acer%20negundo.html

Key learner cues:
- opposite odd-pinnately compound leaves, usually 3 or 5 leaflets
- dioecious tree
- flowers lack petals
- paired winged ovary/fruit
- fruit is two one-seeded samara-like mericarps
- paired samaras separate boxelder from Fraxinus look-alikes

## Range/taxonomy guardrail
The Bexar voucher is A. negundo var. texanum, a currently accepted infraspecific taxon nested within A. negundo. It supports the species-level Acer negundo learner record; do not create a duplicate species from the varietal name.
