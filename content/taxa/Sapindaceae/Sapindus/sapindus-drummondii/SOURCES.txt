## Morphology
UT PRC Williamson County treatment:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Sapindus%20saponaria%20drummondii.html

Native Plant Society of Texas:
https://www.npsot.org/posts/native-plant/sapindus-saponaria-var-drummondii/

Key learner cues:
- alternate once-pinnately compound leaves
- asymmetrical leaflet bases
- terminal leaflet usually absent
- small pale flowers + floral disk
- round translucent yellow/amber one-seeded drupes
- persistent winter fruit clusters

## Taxonomy guardrail
Do not create separate canonical learners for Sapindus drummondii and Sapindus saponaria var. drummondii. Current Kew taxonomy treats the latter as a synonym of the former.
