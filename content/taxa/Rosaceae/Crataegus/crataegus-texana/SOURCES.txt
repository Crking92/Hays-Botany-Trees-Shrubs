TAXONOMY: Kew POWO accepted C. texana Buckley and synonym C. mollis var. texana (Buckley) Lance; recognizes var. texana vs var. dasyphylla.
https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:724380-1
PRIMARY DIAGNOSIS: FNA accepted C. texana: leaves 4–7 cm, lobes 1–4 each side, thick serrations; new twigs woolly; flowers white 14–22 mm in 7–12 flower clusters; 20 ROSE to PURPLE anthers and 4–5 styles; pomes red 9–14(–25) mm with 4–5 PYRENES; straight woody thorns 4–5 cm can be absent.
https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242416358
FNA C. mollis ivory/cream anthers, not pink/purple: https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242416344
FNA C. brazoria 0/little lobed elliptic blades but 20 rose anthers and 4–5 pyrenes: https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=250100070
